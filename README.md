# DARES CDD Hiring Analysis

Analysis of fixed-term hiring (CDD) in France using official DARES labour-market data from 2007–2025.

## Key Findings

- **CDD hiring varies substantially by sector**, ranging from 37.9% to 96.1% of hires in 2025.
- The share of CDDs rose from **74% in 2007 to 88% in 2014–2016**, before declining and partially recovering to **84.6% in 2025**.
- Large establishments had a higher CDD share in 2025 (**88.3%**) than establishments with fewer than 50 employees (**81.4–81.7%**).

## Analysis

The analysis looks at three dimensions of fixed-term hiring:

- **Sector:** Which industries rely most heavily on CDD hiring?
- **Time:** How has the share of CDD hires changed since 2007?
- **Establishment size:** Does CDD reliance differ between small, medium and large establishments?

The Excel workbook contains the source data, analysis tables and a final dashboard summarising the findings.

## Data Source

Source: **DARES — Mouvements de main-d'œuvre (MMO)**.

The data covers private-sector establishments in metropolitan France, excluding agriculture, temporary employment and private household employers. DARES states that the annual series are available from 2007 and can be analysed by sector, establishment size and contract type. 

Official source: [DARES — Les mouvements de main-d’œuvre](https://dares.travail-emploi.gouv.fr/donnees/les-mouvements-de-main-doeuvre)

## Notes

The analysis uses the **share of CDDs among hires**. Historical figures may be affected by methodological revisions to the underlying series.
